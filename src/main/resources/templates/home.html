Frequently Asked Questions
How do I submit a medical claim?
Log in as Employee → click "Submit Claim" → fill the form, upload your PDF bill and medical reports, then submit.
What documents are required?
Original bill (PDF), prescription, medical reports, and doctor's name. Hard copy must also be submitted to Reception.
How long does processing take?
Typically 5–10 working days: 1–2 days at Reception, 2–3 days Medical review, 2–5 days Finance processing.
What does "PENDING" status mean?
Your claim is submitted and waiting for Reception to verify the hard copy. Ensure you have submitted the physical bill.
Can I delete a submitted claim?
Yes — only claims in PENDING or SUBMITTED status can be deleted. Once under review, deletion is not allowed.
Who do I contact for login issues?
Contact your HR department or email help@ccl.co.in with your Employee ID.
